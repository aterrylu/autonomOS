Update from the dashboard. The status-bar pill — now "Update to vY" — opens one screen: a live check of your agents (who's mid-task and what a restart would cost each), what's safe (a snapshot first, automatic rollback, you stay signed in), and every release's notes since your version (the same notes published on GitHub, rendered safely, with any breaking change quoted up top). When every agent is idle it's **Update and restart**; when one is busy the default is **waiting until all agents have been idle for 30 seconds** (or update now — the button names who it interrupts — or cancel). Progress shows in place as three stages. The update runs out of band as its own service job — surviving the restart it causes — with the existing health check and automatic rollback; the dashboard shows progress, reconnects on its own, and reloads onto the new version. Installs not running as a service get the terminal command instead. Before anything changes it saves a snapshot of your agents' setup (`autonomos snapshots list`); afterwards it confirms your agents reopened, and flags any that didn't next to a **Restore v…** action — which, like `autonomos rollback` and the automatic rollback, now restores the previous version *and* its snapshot together. Agents cannot trigger it. (ADR-105)
